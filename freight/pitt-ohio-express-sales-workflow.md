# Pitt-Ohio Express LLC — Third-Party Freight Sales Workflow

## Company
**PITT OHIO** — LTL Trucking, Supply Chain & Logistics Services

## Daily Sales Machine

### Goal
Turn cold third-party leads into repeat freight partners for PITT OHIO.

---

## Step 1: Lead Sourcing (15 min/day)

| Source | What to Extract |
|--------|----------------|
| DAT Load Board | Brokers in your lanes |
| Truckstop.com | Same — filter by lane, load count, reputation |
| LinkedIn Sales Navigator | Logistics managers, broker reps, 3PL owners |
| SaferWatch | Active brokers with authority + credit |
| Old leads | Past quotes that didn't book — call back |

**Target:** 10–15 new leads/day. Company name, contact, phone, email, primary lane.

---

## Step 2: Research (2 min per lead)

Before you call, know:
- Who they are — broker, 3PL, direct shipper
- What lanes they move — match your network
- Load count per week in your lanes
- Credit rating
- Past interactions with PITT OHIO

---

## Step 3: Cold Call Script (45 seconds)

```
"Hey [name], this is [you] with PITT OHIO.

I see you move a lot of [lane, e.g. Pittsburgh → Columbus].
We run that lane daily — trucks positioned and ready.

I'm not asking for a contract. I'm asking for one load.
Give me one shot. If we mess up, you never call me again.
If we nail it, you've got a new option.

What do you have moving today or tomorrow?"
```

**Rules:**
- Name a specific lane
- Say you have trucks NOW
- Ask for ONE load — not a partnership
- No pitch. No volume commitment.

---

## Step 4: Follow-Up Cadence

| Touch | Day | Action |
|-------|-----|--------|
| 1 | Day 1 | Call + email with lane info + your number |
| 2 | Day 3 | Call + text: "Got a truck open tomorrow" |
| 3 | Day 7 | Send one-page rate sheet, 3 sample lanes |
| 4 | Day 14 | Final call: "Circle back. Pause if no interest." |
| — | Day 30 | Move to warm pool. Re-enter weekly scan. |

---

## Step 5: Track Everything

Every call, email, text logged. Required fields per entry:
- Lead name + contact + title
- Date + type (call / email / text)
- One-sentence summary
- Next step + date

---

## Targets

| Metric | Daily Goal |
|--------|-----------|
| Leads sourced | 10–15 |
| Calls made | 20–30 |
| Conversations | 5–8 |
| Loads booked/week | 3–5 |
