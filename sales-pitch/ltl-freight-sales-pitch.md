# WeD0c AI — LTL Freight Sales Enablement

## The LTL Salesperson's Reality

**Pain 1: Lead research takes forever.**
You get a lead. You open six tabs — Google, LinkedIn, SaferWatch, a rate database, their website, maybe a credit report. Twenty minutes later you have the basics. You haven't picked up the phone yet.

**Pain 2: Every email starts from scratch.**
"Hi [name], I noticed [company] ships [lanes]…" — you type the same structure dozens of times a day. No template fits perfectly. You end up copying and pasting from old ones.

**Pain 3: Rate sheets are manual.**
You know the lanes. You know what you can do. But building a clean, competitive rate sheet takes 20 minutes of pulling from spreadsheets, email chains, and memory.

**Pain 4: CRM is a chore.**
The call is done. The rate sheet is sent. Now you have to log it — who you talked to, what was discussed, next steps. That last 10 minutes feels like the hardest.

**Pain 5: Follow-ups fall through.**
"I'll call them in a week." Then it's two weeks. Then the lead is cold. You didn't mean to. It just slipped.

**Pain 6: Load boards don't work for you.**
You scroll through hundreds of posts. Maybe one or two match your lanes. By the time you see it, three other carriers already bid.
